The story of C.P. Lim

At 12, our founder left Swatow, China, for Singapore in search of his Nanyang Dreams. He peddled eggs on the street, then apprenticed under a master wood carver at the former C.K. Tang, mastering teak and camphor wood chest carving.

In 1970, Lim’s Arts & Crafts opened in Holland Road Shopping Centre, and became a household name for eclectic and ethnic lifestyle products.
