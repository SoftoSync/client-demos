A house with a story

Old-world details. A fresh point of view.

Built in the 1870s as a family home, this heritage house is being restored with its original character in mind. The result is a balance of Victorian splendour and modern country chic, with distinctive design around every corner.

Use this space to make the hotel's history, the new ownership and the individual stays easier to discover—without losing the personality that makes Grange Boutique feel different.
