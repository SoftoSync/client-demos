Forty years on Doha’s roads

Mustafawi Rent a Car was established in 1976 and serves business travellers and leisure visitors. The main office is on C-Ring Road, Al-Hilal. Chauffeur-driven cars come from Mustafawi Limousine, and the group also handles real estate rentals and sales.

Demo note: the live site still shows a Kuwait National Day banner (“20% discount from 20th until 29th feb”). In this concept the offers area is a single editable strip, so old promotions are easy to retire.
