Your neighborhood club for the whole family

Whether you are a seasoned athlete or just starting out, Mt. Tam provides a welcoming and supportive environment to achieve your goals. A full-service tennis and fitness club with indoor and outdoor tennis courts, heated swimming pools, a full gym, an indoor basketball court and an onsite cafe.

  • A variety of high-quality tennis, swimming and fitness programs
  • A friendly and inclusive atmosphere where everyone feels welcome
  • A love of sports and fitness in our community
