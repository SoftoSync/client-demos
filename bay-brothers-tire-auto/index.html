Meet the team dedicated to keeping you safe on the road

Bay Brothers Tire & Auto LLC was founded by brothers Luis and Jose Prato with a clear vision: to provide honest, professional tire and auto services to the San Francisco community. What started as a small family operation has grown into a trusted name in automotive care.

Our reputation is built on quality workmanship, transparent pricing, and genuine care for every customer who walks through our doors.

Today, we operate with state-of-the-art equipment, a highly skilled team of certified technicians, and partnerships with leading tire and parts manufacturers. We’re proud to be your neighborhood auto service experts.
